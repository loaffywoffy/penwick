Everything you need

A full writing studio

Not a notes app pretending to be a manuscript. Penwick is built for long-form work, from first line to final export.

Real paginated pages

Your words flow across separate page-sized sheets, just like Word or Pages — add pages as you write.

Rich text, done right

Every system font in its own typeface, sizes, bold/italic/underline, colour, alignment, lists, and full undo.

Version history

Penwick auto-snapshots your drafts as you go. Roll back to any earlier version, anytime.

Character name generator

Spin up gender-matched first names with surnames from real per-country databases.

Live collaboration

Write together through iCloud — see who's here and watch edits sync between people.

Export anywhere

PDF, DOCX, Markdown, RTF, plain text and Fountain — for editors, agents, or your screenplay tools.

Themes & dark mode

Seven colour themes plus light, dark and auto — make the page yours.

Focus mode

Hide everything but the page and write with nothing between you and the words.

Auto-updates

New versions install themselves straight from GitHub — you're always on the latest.

Is it a virus? No. Here's the honest truth.

When you first open Penwick, macOS may warn you that it's from an "unidentified developer." That is not because anything is wrong with it — it's because I haven't paid Apple's $99/year Developer Program fee to code-sign the app. I'm one person building this for fun, and I genuinely can't justify $99 a year just to remove a scary dialog.

So instead of asking you to trust me, I'm showing you everything. Penwick is fully open source. Every single line of code is public — read it, build it yourself, audit it for anything sketchy. There's no telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no servers. Your writing lives in your own iCloud Drive as plain .rtf files.

How to install

Because the app isn't code-signed, macOS needs you to open it once the manual way. After that it just works — and updates itself.

1

Download & unzip

Grab the zip below and double-click it. Drag Penwick.app into your Applications folder.

2

Right-click → Open

The first time only, right-click Penwick and choose Open, then click Open in the dialog.

3

Start writing

That's it. Penwick keeps itself up to date from here — no need to come back to this page.
