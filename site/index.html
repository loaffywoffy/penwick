Honest, by design

Is it a virus? No.

When you first open Penwick, macOS may say it's from an "unidentified developer." That's not a problem with the app — it's because I haven't paid Apple's $99/year fee to code-sign it. One person, building this for the love of it.

So instead of asking you to trust me, I show you everything. Penwick is fully open source — read every line, build it yourself, audit it. No telemetry, no accounts, no servers. Your writing stays in your own iCloud as plain .rtf files.
